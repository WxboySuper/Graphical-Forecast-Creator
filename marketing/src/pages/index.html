Create forecasts.Your way.

A forecast builder for weather people. Make your own products, draw them on the map across an eight-day cycle, explain your reasoning, and check how it verified.

Create Account Continue without account →
01 / Plan

Pick the day. Pick the product.

Choose a day in your cycle, Day 1 through 8. Then start the severe outlook that comes built in, or press + to create your own custom layer.

  • Days 1–8 in one cycle
  • The severe outlook: Tor, Wind, Hail, Cat
  • Custom layers with your own categories
02 / Build

Draw it on the map.

Pick a category and draw polygons. Your legend builds itself as you go.

  • Pan, Draw, Delete, undo and redo
  • Custom colors and opacity per category
  • Save to your library of products
03 / Explain

Explain your reasoning.

Write freeform in the DIY editor, or answer the Guided questions and watch a formatted discussion build in the live preview.

  • Valid times and forecaster name
  • Guided sections: summary, setup, expectations
  • Export when you're done
04 / Monitor

Monitor the day.

Bring up radar, satellite, NWS watches and warnings, and SPC storm reports. Dark mode is one tap away.

  • MRMS radar and GOES satellite
  • NWS warning polygons and SPC reports
  • Light and dark themes
05 / Verify

See what held up.

Load your forecast package and grade it against SPC storm reports and NOAA DAT surveys. You get a score out of 100.

  • Hazard-by-hazard breakdown
  • Evidence you can inspect on the map

A place to make your own forecasts, then check them.

GFC is a forecast builder for weather enthusiasts, students, and forecasters who want practice. You make the call. The reports grade it.

What it is

  • A builder for your own forecast products
  • A way to explain and share your reasoning
  • A practice loop: plan, build, monitor, verify

What it isn't

  • An official forecast or warning source
  • Something to make safety decisions with
  • Affiliated with the NWS or SPC
