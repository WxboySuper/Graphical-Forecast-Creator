Bring your own process. Make GFC fit it.

If you already know how you forecast, GFC gets out of your way. Define your own products, analyze the environment, and hold your forecasts to a standard you can inspect.

Custom products

Define the categories.

Create a layer for anything you forecast, name every category, and set its color and opacity. Up to twelve reusable categories per hosted product, with snapshots, exports, and imports.

Multi-day cycles

Eight days, one cycle.

Build Day 1 through Day 8 in one place, copy geometry between days or hazards, and keep a history of earlier cycles to compare against.

Mesoscale workspace

Analyze, then annotate.

HRRR, RAP, and RRFS fields by run and forecast hour, including CAPE, helicity, and composite reflectivity. Draw hazard-styled polygons, circles, ellipses, and corridors, reshape them by vertex from the keyboard, and write the discussion alongside.

Discussion

Your format, your words.

Use the DIY editor for freeform writing with valid times and your name, or the Guided editor for structure. Edit the map and discussion side by side, then review and export.

Verification

A score you can read.

Grade against SPC storm reports and NOAA DAT surveys with source-aware evidence, report-quality checks, probability skill, and false-alarm discipline. The methodology is published in the app.
