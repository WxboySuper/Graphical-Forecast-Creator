Pricing questions.

Yes. The forecast builder, discussion editor, monitor, verification, local autosave, and exports are free, with or without an account. Anything that costs money to operate, like cloud storage, is what Premium covers.

Your local work stays fully available. Only new cloud writes are turned off, and everything you saved stays yours.

Hosting cloud storage costs real money. Premium lets the people who want it help cover that cost, so core forecasting never has to go behind a paywall.
