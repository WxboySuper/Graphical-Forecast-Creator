The practice loop

Plan. Build. Monitor. Verify.

Good forecasters get better by closing the loop: make a call, watch it play out, and check honestly how it did. GFC puts every step of that loop in one place.

  1. Plan

    Pick the day and the product you are forecasting.

  2. Build

    Draw your areas and probabilities on the map.

  3. Explain

    Write down why, in your own words or with guided prompts.

  4. Monitor

    Watch radar, warnings, and reports as the day unfolds.

  5. Verify

    Grade the forecast against what actually happened.

Good to know: Forecasts made in GFC are unofficial and are not for safety decisions. For official forecasts and warnings, always use the National Weather Service at weather.gov.
