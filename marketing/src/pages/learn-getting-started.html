Make your first forecast.

You do not need an account or any experience. This walkthrough takes you from a blank map to a graded forecast.

Open the App

  1. Pick the day and the product

    Open the Forecast workspace and choose a day in your cycle. Start with the severe outlook that comes built in: tornado, wind, and hail probabilities, plus an automatic categorical map.

  2. Draw an area

    Choose a category such as 5% tornado, switch to Draw, and click out a polygon around where you expect storms. Higher probabilities nest inside lower ones. Made a mistake? Undo and try again.

  3. Explain your thinking

    Open the discussion. The Guided editor asks about the overall threat, the meteorological setup, and what you expect, then formats it for you. Writing down why is the fastest way to learn.

  4. Watch the day unfold

    Switch to Monitor to see radar, satellite, warnings, and storm reports on top of your forecast. Use Playback to see how the day evolved.

  5. Grade it the next morning

    Load your forecast package in Verification. GFC scores it out of 100 against storm reports and damage surveys and shows where you were right and where you missed.

Start small

Forecast just one hazard for one region. A focused forecast is easier to learn from than a perfect one.

Compare to the pros

Read the official outlook from the Storm Prediction Center after you draw yours, then ask what they saw that you did not.

Keep going

Forecast every day for a week. Your grade trends show you where you are improving.

Reminder: Forecasts made in GFC are unofficial. For decisions that affect safety, use official sources at weather.gov.

Ready when you are.

Start without an account. Create one later to sync your settings.
