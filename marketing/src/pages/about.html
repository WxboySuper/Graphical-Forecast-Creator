A modern place to learn by forecasting.

Graphical Forecast Creator gives weather enthusiasts, students, and forecasters the tools to build forecasts, watch them play out, and reflect on how they did.

Why it exists

Practicing forecasting should not mean improvising.

Learning to forecast used to mean sketching outlooks over screenshots, wrestling with tools that were never built for the job, or hunting for something approachable enough for a student.

GFC puts you in the driver's seat of the kind of forecasts you see almost every day, with a modern tool made for exactly that.

Core is free

If a feature is core to forecasting, it is free. Premium only pays for the things that cost money to run.

Open source

All of the code is public, so you can read it, learn from it, and build on it.

Shaped by its users

Releases, fixes, and plans come from the feedback of the people who use GFC.

Who builds it

One developer, many contributors of ideas.

GFC is built and maintained by Alex (WeatherboySuper), a meteorology student, with a lot of help from the community that tests it and tells him what to fix next.

An educational tool. GFC is not an official source of watches, warnings, or forecasts, and is not affiliated with the National Weather Service, the Storm Prediction Center, or NOAA. For official information, use weather.gov.
