Feedback and bugs

Signed in to the app? Use Send feedback in the Resources menu. It captures what you were doing and goes straight to the project. Otherwise, email us with what happened and what you expected.

Billing

Questions about Premium, receipts, or cancelling? Email us from the address on your account and we will sort it out. Payments are handled by Stripe.

Privacy and data requests

To access, correct, or delete your data, email us. You can also delete your account from the Account page in the app. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Community

Join the GFC Discord to ask questions, share forecasts, and talk with other forecasters.

Developers and contributors

GFC is open source. Browse the code, open an issue, or send a pull request on GitHub.

GFC is a small project, so replies can take a few days. For anything involving weather safety, please use official sources at weather.gov.
