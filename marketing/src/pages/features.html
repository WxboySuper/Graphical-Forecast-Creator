Everything a forecast needs. In one place.

Plan the day, draw the product, explain your thinking, watch it unfold, and grade the result. Each step has its own workspace, and they all share the same forecast.

Forecast

Draw it on the map.

Pick a category, draw a polygon, and your legend builds itself. The severe outlook comes built in with tornado, wind, and hail probabilities, hatching for significant threats, and an automatic categorical map.

  • An eight-day cycle, Day 1 through Day 8
  • Pan, draw, delete, undo, and redo
  • Autosave on your device, with a history of earlier cycles

Custom layers

Make the product you wish existed.

Heavy rain, fire weather, winter precipitation, tropical areas of interest: create a layer, name it, and give every category its own color and opacity. Save your products to your library and reuse them.

  • Your own categories, colors, and legends
  • Reusable products you can import and export
  • Built-in Rainfall and Tropical products for everyone

Discussion

Explain your reasoning.

Write freeform in the DIY editor, or answer the Guided questions and watch a formatted discussion build in the live preview. Edit the map and the words side by side in the severe discussion workspace.

  • Valid times and your forecaster name
  • Guided sections for summary, setup, and expectations
  • Review and export when you are done

Monitor

Watch the day happen.

Bring up MRMS radar and GOES satellite, NWS watches and warnings, and SPC storm reports beside your forecast. Playback shows how the day evolved. Dark mode is one tap away.

  • Radar and satellite with freshness labels
  • Warning polygons and storm reports
  • Your forecast stays on the map

Verification

See what held up.

Load your forecast and grade it against SPC storm reports and NOAA Damage Assessment Toolkit surveys. You get a score out of 100, a hazard-by-hazard breakdown, and evidence you can inspect on the map.

  • Probability skill and false-alarm discipline
  • Shareable summaries and grade trends
  • A methodology you can read in full

Mesoscale · New in v2

Analyze the setup before the storms.

Browse HRRR, RAP, and RRFS model fields by run and forecast hour: CAPE, helicity, composite reflectivity, and more. Draw polygons, circles, ellipses, and corridors on top, add MRMS and GOES context, and write a mesoscale discussion.

  • Readable layer names, run and hour selectors
  • Hazard-styled areas you can reshape vertex by vertex
  • Keeps working when model data is unavailable
