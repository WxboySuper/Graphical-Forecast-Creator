GFC marketing homepage

Four directions for gfcweather.com. Each one has the same copy and the same two hover-expand doors, so you can compare the visual language directly. Hover or tap a door in each to test the interaction.

  • Immersive scroll build (v2)The expanded direction. A pinned map draws a forecast as you scroll, the two doors go full-screen, and a plain-language section explains what GFC does and what it is not.
  • A. Split DoorsWarm paper and storm-blue palette. Editorial and calm. The closest to the spec as written.
  • B. Paint the MapInteractive hero: visitors paint a practice risk area before choosing a door. The most hands-on option.
  • C. Learning TrailLight, friendly, with soft color blobs and a four-step Draw, Discuss, Monitor, Verify path that reveals on scroll.
  • D. Radar DeskDark, monitor-style. A radar sweep and mono labels. The option most likely to appeal to experienced users.
